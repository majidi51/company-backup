Privacy Policy

Company Backup Rclone is a private-use application for transferring scheduled server backups to an authorized Google Drive account using Rclone.

The application accesses Google Drive only after authorization by the account owner, for storing and managing backups.

Backup files may contain business information and are stored in the account owner's Google Drive.

The application does not sell data or use it for advertising.

The account owner controls backup retention, deletion, and access permissions.
